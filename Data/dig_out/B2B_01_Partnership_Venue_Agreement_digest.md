SUMMARY  
This is a non-exclusive partnership venue agreement between Shack Entertainment Limited and a third-party venue, governing the venue’s participation in the Company’s event network for a 12-month term. The Venue is responsible for premises, staff, licensing, and safety, while the Company handles production, promotion, and ticketing (unless otherwise specified in the Event Order). Revenue splits are defined in Event Orders, with bar/catering revenue going to the Venue and artist merchandise to the artist. Insurance and branding rights are clearly delineated, with each party responsible for their own liability and branding usage.

PARTIES  
- Shack Entertainment Limited (“the Company”)  
- [VENUE NAME], of [address] (“the Venue”)

MONEY  
- Net ticket revenue split per Event Order (defaulted to Rate Card).  
- Bar and catering revenue: Venue’s.  
- Artist merchandise revenue: Artist’s (no cut to either party).  
- Settlement: Statement within 7 days of event; payment follows statement.

DATES & DEADLINES  
- Term: 12 months; renewal by mutual agreement; 30-day notice required.  
- Event confirmation: One-page Event Order (date, capacity, ticket price, split)  
- Settlement: Within 7 days post-event

OBLIGATIONS  
- Venue: Premises, staff, licensing, safety compliance, dressing room access, power access.  
- Company: Production to house standard, promotion via Shack media, ticketing (unless specified otherwise).

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- Material breaches include violations of licensing or safeguarding laws.  
- No liability for each party’s equipment or public liability is shared; each insures own assets.  
- No recourse for Company if venue fails to meet safety or compliance obligations.  
- Venue’s responsibility for artist merchandise revenue (no split) may create confusion or dispute if artist revenue is not clearly tracked.

KEY TERMS  
- Non-exclusive: both parties.  
- Event Order: binding confirmation of event, split, and conditions.  
- Rate Card: default revenue split unless overridden.  
- AU_01 clauses: 9.1-9.8 apply (external contractual terms).  
- Brand Guidelines: Venue may use Shack logo and name subject to those rules.
