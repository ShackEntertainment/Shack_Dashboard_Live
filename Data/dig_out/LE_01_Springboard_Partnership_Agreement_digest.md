_Chewed 2026-08-30 by the local 4B._

SUMMARY  
This is a one-year partnership agreement between Shack Entertainment Limited and an artist, designed as a springboard for the Artist’s career. The Company handles bookings, production, and promotion through its media channels, while the Artist retains full control and earns 70% of gross ticket revenue (or as stipulated in engagement orders). Termination occurs upon graduation to a major deal or for material breach, with specific financial and behavioral obligations for both parties. IP rights are clearly assigned: the Artist owns their own material, while the Company owns all Shack-produced content with a perpetual, royalty-free license granted to the Artist for self-promotion.

PARTIES  
Shack Entertainment Limited (company no. 14628241, 25 Fielding Avenue, Twickenham, England, TW2 5LX); [Artist Name], residing at [address].

MONEY  
Default split: 70% Artist, 30% Company. All funds held in Company’s client account (Santander). Monthly statements issued within 7 days, payment follows. Artist’s consent required for Company expenses over £50/month. Success Fee: 15% of new deal advances/guaranteed earnings (due within 30 days).

DATES & DEADLINES  
- Agreement term: 1 year.  
- Graduation: Artist may terminate with 60 days’ notice if signed major deal/agency.  
- Material breach: 14-day cure period; repeated unexcused no-shows are breaches.  
- Cancellation: Company must cancel 15+ days prior to forfeit 50%; 7 days or less = 100%. Artist cancels within 14 days = forfeit fee + Company costs.  
- Settlement: 30 days after termination.

OBLIGATIONS  
**Company:** Book, produce, promote, account transparently; provide venue, equipment, insurance, catering, security, and dressing room per Schedule 2.  
**Artist:** Available for rotation; perform 90-120 min professional sets; provide promotional materials; ensure non-infringing material; comply with venue safety rules; conduct professionally.  
**Engagement capping:** 6 per quarter unless written agreement. No slot binding until signed Engagement Order.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- Artist must be available and perform consistently; failure to attend or cancel without notice forfeits fee.  
- Success Fee may be substantial if Artist signs a lucrative deal.  
- Company may incur losses if Artist cancels short notice or fails to perform.  
- Company’s costs must be authorized by Artist for expenses >£50/month.  
- IP assignment may be viewed as a risk: Company owns all “Shack Productions” — Artist has limited rights to use for self-promotion.

KEY TERMS  
- Rotation Year: 1 year  
- Success Fee: 15% of new deal earnings  
- Engagement Order (LE_02): Required to bind slots  
- Shack Productions: All recordings, photos, materials produced by/for Company  
- Underlying Works: Artist’s original creations  
- Material Breach: Failure to cure within 14 days  
- Door Deal: Engagements with fixed fees or revenue share  
- SIA-accredited Security: Mandatory for events
