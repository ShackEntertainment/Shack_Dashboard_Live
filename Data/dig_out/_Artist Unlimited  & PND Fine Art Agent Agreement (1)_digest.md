**SUMMARY**  
This agreement establishes a personal representation arrangement between Paul Nicholas Duncan (as individual Artist) and Bola Kassim, acting as agent for Artists Unlimited, for the exclusive representation of the Artist in all entertainment industry matters. The Agent acts as the Artist’s personal agent, negotiating and executing agreements, and is entitled to 30% commission of the Artist’s gross earnings — defined broadly as all income received, regardless of deductions — from all sales, assignments, or transactions secured. Funds are to be held in a trust account by the Agent and remitted to the Artist or as directed, with the Artist obligated to direct any third-party payments to the Agent. The term is initially five years, with automatic 12-month renewals unless terminated 60 business days prior. The Artist must return samples within 30 days of termination, with liability for damage limited to negligence. The Agent may advance funds and retain expenses, payable only after the Artist earns revenue, and must issue monthly statements and reimbursements within seven days. The relationship is explicitly independent contractor, not partnership or employment, with no warranties of success, and subject to English law. Termination is at either party’s discretion with 60 days’ notice, and no clauses may be enforced if invalid, with the remainder of the agreement remaining enforceable.

**PARTIES**

**MONEY**  
- Commission: 30% of the Artist’s gross earnings from all sales, assignments, and transactions secured by the Agent  
- “Gross earnings” includes all monies or considerations received, regardless of deductions or exclusions  
- Funds must be held in a separate trust account by the Agent and remitted to the Artist or as directed  
- Artist is responsible for directing all third-party payments to the Agent, who must then remit commissions and cover expenses  
- Reimbursements: Artist must reimburse the Agent for expenses within 7 business days of receipt of invoice or statement  
- Advances: Agent may make advances for the Artist’s benefit, repayable only upon Artist’s earnings in the entertainment industry  
- Late payments: 5% monthly interest charged if not paid within 7 business days of invoice
