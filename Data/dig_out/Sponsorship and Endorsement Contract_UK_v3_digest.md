**SUMMARY**  
This agreement outlines a sponsorship arrangement between the Sponsor and Shack Entertainment (the Manager and Artist’s representative), for the “The Live Exchange Shows” event. The Sponsor receives branding rights, merchandise opportunities, and promotional support, while Shack Entertainment assumes obligations such as providing signage, marketing support, and ensuring proper insurance coverage. The Sponsor may purchase up to 10% of tickets per venue at face value and is restricted from endorsing Manager’s products or listing other sponsors. The agreement is governed by English and Welsh law and includes provisions for joint liability, Force Majeure, and confidentiality. Specific dates, payment amounts, and termination conditions remain undefined and require formalization.

**PARTIES**  
- Sponsor (represented by signed Manager)  
- Shack Entertainment (represented by signed officer; Artist is jointly and severally liable with Shack Entertainment)

**MONEY**  
- Payment: Amount unspecified; additional marketing fees (TV commercials, point-of-sale, press, appearances) to be paid by Sponsor, “made available” without specific figures  
- Sponsor may purchase up to 10% of tickets per venue at face value

**DATES & DEADLINES**  
- Event dates: Not specified  
- Press conference: Not specified  
- Agreement term: Commences with execution; ends conditionally upon performance or termination; no fixed expiration date

**OBLIGATIONS**  
- **Sponsor**:  
  - Must be (part or exclusive) sponsor of the Event  
  - Must not list or mention other parties as sponsors or presenters  
  - Must approve logo/catchphrase, provide branded banners, promote merchandise  
  - Must not authorize Artist to endorse Sponsor’s products or grant licenses  
  - Must cooperate on pre-production materials and commercial use  
  - Must provide only Sponsor’s products backstage; cover endorsement costs (airfare, accommodation, etc.)  
  - Must ensure confidentiality and no third-party disclosure  
  - Must comply with performance and conduct obligations  
- **Shack Entertainment**:  
  - Must provide venue signage, merchandise approvals, marketing support  
  - Must indemnify Sponsor for merchandise liability  
  - Must ensure event staff wear Sponsor’s custom apparel/devices  
  - Must reproduce Sponsor’s logo on Artist’s merchandise (subject to Manager/Record Label approval)  
  - Must ensure Artist and Event have appropriate insurance  
  - Must handle Force Majeure suspensions per industry standards

**RISKS & RED FLAGS**  
- No obligation to advertise — Sponsor may choose not to promote  
- Sponsor’s merchandise may be viewed as competing with Manager’s merchandise  
- Manager must ensure Artist does not endorse Sponsor’s products  
- No breach penalties or exclusivity beyond Event  
- No defined termination clause beyond “Expiration of this Agreement”  
- No mention of liability for breach or damages  
- Force Majeure may suspend agreement without clear definition or triggers  
- Reputation damage may justify termination for either party  
- Joint and several liability between Artist and Shack Entertainment  
- No warranties on Sponsor’s products or Artist’s conduct (except in “controversy” clause)  
- Trademark protection: Sponsor’s logo cannot be used post-termination without agreement

**KEY TERMS**  
- Sponsorship: Exclusive or part sponsorship of Event  
- Endorsement: Sponsor’s branding rights in marketing materials  
- Merchandise: Sponsor may offer T-shirts/posters with Artist/Event branding  
- Territory: U.S., U.K., Europe, Canada, Japan (customizable)  
- Exclusivity: Sponsor has “right of first negotiation” for TV specials  
- Indemnity: Sponsor indemnifies Manager/Artist for merchandise  
- Property: All materials and logos owned by Shack Entertainment unless music/lyrics owned by Sponsor  
- Joint and Several Liability: Artist + Shack Entertainment jointly liable  
- Confidentiality: No third-party disclosure  
- Governed by: English and Welsh law  
- Force Majeure: May suspend agreement per industry standards  
- Termination: For reputation damage or Force Majeure (no clear triggers or procedures)
