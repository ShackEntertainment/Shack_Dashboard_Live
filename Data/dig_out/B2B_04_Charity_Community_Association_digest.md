# Digest — B2B_04_Charity_Community_Association.md
_Chewed 2026-08-30 by the local 4B._

SUMMARY  
This document establishes a non-revenue, collaborative framework between Shack Entertainment Limited and a registered charity for community-focused activities, governed by simple written orders. Neither party has financial obligations or exclusivity rights; all rights, permissions, and branding are limited to confirmed activities and expire upon 30-day notice. The agreement emphasizes compliance with safeguarding laws and does not create partnership, joint venture, or employment relationships.

PARTIES  
Shack Entertainment Limited (the Company) and [Charity Name], registered charity no. [number].

MONEY  
None. No financial obligations or payments are included.

DATES & DEADLINES  
30 days’ notice required for either party to terminate; all brand/logo use ceases immediately upon exit. No specific deadlines beyond written orders and notice periods.

OBLIGATIONS  
- Company: Provide discounted or pro-bono performance slots, promotional support, approved archive content.  
- Charity: Provide community outreach, co-promotion, consent for approved campaign use of its name/logo.  
- Both: Comply with safeguarding laws; Charity warrants its registered status.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- No exclusivity or financial obligations may lead to misunderstandings.  
- “No revenue contract” clause may be misinterpreted as no obligation to contribute.  
- No partnership or employment status — could lead to liability issues if mislabeled.  
- “Exit” clause may be ambiguous if “written order” is not clearly defined or enforced.  
- Dependency on “approved campaign only” for logo/name use creates scope for misuse if not properly vetted.

KEY TERMS  
- Framework for collaboration, not revenue contract  
- “Written order” for each activity confirmation  
- No exclusivity or employment status  
- 30-day notice for exit  
- “Approved campaign only” for charity branding  
- “Safeguarding law compliance” required
