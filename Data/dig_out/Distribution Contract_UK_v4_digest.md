SUMMARY  
This agreement establishes a non-exclusive distribution arrangement between an Artist and a Distributor, whereby the Distributor receives a percentage of the Dealer Price (PPD) per unit sold in a defined Territory, subject to the Artist’s ownership rights and compliance with royalties and licensing obligations. The Artist is responsible for securing all necessary permissions and royalty payments, while the Distributor handles sales, delivery, and collection — retaining rights to commercialize post-termination. Payments are quarterly unless thresholds are met, with audit rights and retention of 70–80% of revenue for services rendered. Termination is limited, with artist-initiated terminations being unconditional, and distributor-initiated terminations requiring 30 days’ notice. Both parties must remain personally bound and cannot transfer obligations without consent. Disputes are governed by English law, and prior discussions are superseded by this document.

PARTIES  
Artist: [Name of artist], registered at [artist’s address]  
Distributor: [Name of distributor], registered at [address or operational location]  
Both parties must sign and provide addresses. The agreement is personal and not transferable without prior written consent.

MONEY  
Distributor pays Artist a percentage (to be confirmed) of the Dealer Price (PPD) per unit sold in Territory, excluding returned or unsold units. Artist may prepay shipping/handling for initial shipments, and Distributor pays for returns/unsold units. Distributor may pay mechanical royalties on behalf of Artist if agreed. Payments are quarterly if aggregate due exceeds £200; otherwise, payments are based on Distributor’s accounting statements. Distributor may retain 70–80% of revenue for services rendered, and must remit underpayments exceeding 10% of audit findings plus audit costs. All payments are subject to Distributor’s accounting records and audit rights.

DATES & DEADLINES  
Term: [X] years, auto-renews unless Artist gives 3-month written notice to terminate. Distributor may terminate with 30-day written notice. Distributor may terminate immediately if Recordings contain unlicensed samples or Artist lacks ownership rights. Initial shipment must be delivered within 21 days of agreement. Quarterly statements due 30 days after quarter end. Audits: Once per year at Artist’s request, covering only the most recent 12-month period; no prior audits. Breach cure period: 30 days after notice. No effective date change unless written, signed, and dated amendment is made.

OBLIGATIONS  
Distributor: Solicit and service Licensees; encode Recordings as required; deliver to Licensees; collect payments; retain rights to distribute and commercialize for 6 months post-termination.  
Artist: Secure and pay all mechanical, publishing, synchronization, digital mechanical, and sample royalties; ensure ownership and clear rights to Recordings; “drop ship” initial shipment of [number] units within 21 days; prepay shipping/handling for shipments; notify Distributor of any third-party distribution.  
Neither party may terminate for breach unless it results in damages, not penalty. No joint liability or partnership status. No transfers without consent. All amendments require written, signed, dated terms.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
Artist liable for copyright infringement, sample clearance, and royalty obligations. Distributor may terminate immediately for unlicensed content or lack of ownership rights. Distributor does not guarantee retailer placement. Artist retains full ownership but grants exclusive/non-exclusive distribution rights. No worldwide rights unless specified; distributor’s access may be limited. Artist must notify Distributor of third-party distribution (especially non-exclusive). Distributor may underpay >10% if audit finds discrepancies — requires immediate remittance + costs. No prior audits — potential disputes over past due payments. Agreement is personal and not transferable — no enforcement if term is deemed illegal. Breach does not permit termination — only damages.
