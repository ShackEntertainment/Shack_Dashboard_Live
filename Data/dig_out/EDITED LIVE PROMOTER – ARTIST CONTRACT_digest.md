SUMMARY  
This agreement outlines the terms between the Artist (including Key Member, Musicians, and Crew) and the Promoter, Shack Entertainment Ltd, for a live performance engagement. The Artist is responsible for performance, compliance with regulations, and personal expenses, while the Promoter arranges venue, backline, safety, catering, and insurance. Payment is structured in two installments, with cancellation penalties and force majeure provisions. Key obligations include mutual safety compliance, IP indemnification, and non-transferability of rights. Legal enforceability, insurance coverage, and schedule completeness are pending finalization.

PARTIES  
Artist: Includes Key Member, up to 7 Accompanying Musicians, and Crew (Tour Manager, Security).  
Promoter: Shack Entertainment Ltd, with registered office provided.

MONEY  
Total fee: £______ payable in two installments — 50% upon arrival at Venue; 50% upon satisfactory completion of Engagement. Artist bears all taxes, travel, accommodation, and daily expenses. Promoter covers promotional costs, royalties, fines, and intellectual property protection.

DATES & DEADLINES  
Engagement date: ____________ (Schedule 1).  
Cancellation: 15+ days prior = 50% fee due; 7 days or less prior = 100% fee due.  
Performance duration: Minimum 1h 30m, maximum 2h (unless otherwise agreed).  
Rehearsal: Venue available prior to Engagement for sound-check/practice.

OBLIGATIONS  
Artist:  
- Perform professionally and punctually; provide promotional materials, CV, biography, masters, photos; indemnify Promoter; avoid unauthorized expenditure; allow interviews prior/post Engagement.  
- Comply with health, safety, fire, and venue regulations.  

Promoter:  
- Arrange Venue, rehearsal, backline (Schedule 2), travel/accommodation (if requested), safety compliance, insurance, public liability coverage.  
- Use Artist’s IP for non-exclusive promotion.  
- Provide: climate-controlled dressing room, catering (water, wine, juice, tea, hot dinner 2h pre-show), technical setup (sound/lighting/staff/power), SIA-accredited security.  
- Share equipment agreement with Artist; non-transferable, personal agreement binding on parties.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- Artist liable for damages, legal claims, or IP infringement from own content.  
- Promoter’s liability limited for Artist’s injury or property damage (unless due to negligence, recklessness, or omissions).  
- Artist’s breach may result in full reimbursement of Promoter costs.  
- Force Majeure: May extend obligations with mutual agreement and notice.  
- No third-party enforceability (Clause 9(c)).  
- No clauses on liability, insurance, indemnification, breach penalties, or dispute resolution — high risk of enforcement gap.  
- Artist’s obligations not tied to performance metrics or financial triggers.

KEY TERMS  
- “Artist”: Key Member, Musicians, Crew (Tour Manager, Security).  
- “Promoter”: Shack Entertainment Ltd.  
- “Engagement”: Performance at Venue (Schedule 1).  
- “Backline”: Defined in Schedule 2, Section 3.  
- “Force Majeure”: Events beyond control (e.g., strikes, war, fire, government actions).  
- “Venue”: Live Exchange, with capacity and address to be filled.  
- “Intellectual Property”: Artist must confirm all materials are true and non-infringing.  
- “Personal to parties, not transferable without Artist’s written consent.”  
- “Severability clause”: Invalid provisions are severed, not voiding entire agreement.  
- “Supersedes all prior agreements.”  
- “Rider details in Schedule 2.”  
- “Engagement governed by English courts.”  
- “No variation without written consent of all parties.”
