# Digest — AU_01_Fine_Art_Agency_Agreement.md
_Chewed 2026-08-30 by the local 4B._

**SUMMARY**  
This is a non-exclusive agency agreement between Shack Entertainment Limited and an Artist for the placement, exhibition, and sale of the Artist’s works via the Company’s channels. The Company earns a 30% commission on gross sales during the 3-year term, which drops to 15%, 10%, and 5% after each year post-termination. All proceeds are held in trust for the Artist, with monthly statements issued and payments made within 30 days. The Artist retains full ownership and copyright, and the Company bears all risks, shipping, and insurance for consigned works. Termination is either by written notice after 3 months or immediately for material breach, with 30-day return of unsold works. No partnership, employment, or agency of law is created. Disputes are mediated first, then litigated in England and Wales.

**MONEY**

- All proceeds held in trust with Santander plc account  
- Monthly statements issued within 7 days; payments within 30 days of sale  
- Layaway/installment sales >6 months require Artist’s written consent  
- Company expenses >£50/month require Artist’s written consent  

**DATES & DEADLINES**  
- Term: 3 years from agreement date (no automatic renewal)  
- Termination notice: 3 months’ written notice or immediate for material breach  
- Cure period for breach: 14 days from written notice  
- Final payment & return of unsold works: within 30 days of termination  
- Monthly statements: issued within 7 days of month-end  

**OBLIGATIONS**  
- Artist consigns works; Company retains no title or copyright  
- Company bears return shipping and insurance for unsold/withdrawn works  
- Artist pays shipping costs to Company; bears risk of loss/thief/damage to consigned works  
- Company must insure consigned works and keep logs with SKU/title/retail price  
- Retail pricing and discounts >10% require Artist’s written approval  
- Company may use works at partner outlets under same trust terms  
- Company must notify buyers Artist retains reproduction rights  

**RISKS & RED FLAGS**  
- No automatic renewal; termination is easy via notice or breach  
- Artist retains full copyright and title — Company has no ownership  
- Company bears full risk of consigned works (insurance required)  
- No power of attorney or agency of law — independent contractor relationship  
- No third-party rights under the Act; limited use of artwork for promotion  
- No clause allowing for force majeure, business interruption, or credit risk mitigation
