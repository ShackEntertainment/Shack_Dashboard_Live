SUMMARY  
This consignment agreement between an artist and a gallery outlines the terms for the gallery to represent and sell the artist’s paintings, including commission structure, payment terms, responsibilities for frame and artwork, and conditions for withdrawal or termination. The artist retains copyright and title until paid, and the gallery assumes full liability for loss, damage, or theft of consigned works. Payment is due within 30 days of sale, with installment or layaway terms subject to artist consent. The gallery must return unsold works and retain copies of sales invoices confidentially.

MONEY  
- Artist receives: Retail price minus recipient’s commission (percentage to be filled).  
- Installment payments: Artist receives monthly % (to be filled) until full payment.  
- Layaway sales: Max 6 months without artist consent.  
- Payment due: 30 days after sale; 30 days after termination for final collection.  
- Shipping: Artist pays for sending works to recipient; recipient pays return shipping and insurance.

DATES & DEADLINES  
- 30 days: to notify termination or withdraw works; to pay artist after sale; to collect works after termination.  
- Consignment agreements: provided with each new work.  
- Invoice copies: attached with remittance.

OBLIGATIONS  
- Gallery: Represent artist, hold works in trust, pay artist upon sale, assume liability for artwork/frame, repair/replace frames, return unsold works, return frame if customer declines, display only consigned works, notify buyers of artist’s reproduction rights, keep sales info confidential, not display consigned vs owned works.  
- Artist: Provide original, non-libelous, non-infringing works, pay shipping to recipient, retain copyright, permit gallery to represent.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- Gallery liable for loss, theft, damage or destruction of consigned works.  
- Artist retains title until paid, but must ship works to gallery; gallery bears return shipping/insurance costs.  
- Frame cost may be deducted up to 10% if customer declines frame — commission recalculated.  
- Artist may withdraw works with 30 days’ notice; gallery may return works without notice.  
- No protection from gallery’s creditors; works held in trust but artist remains responsible for shipping to recipient.  
- No express warranty on artwork quality — artist warrants only authenticity, copyright compliance, and no libel.

KEY TERMS  
- Consignment: Artist allows gallery to sell works, retaining title and copyright.  
- Commission: Percentage to be filled, withheld from artist’s retail price.  
- Retained Title: Artist retains ownership until paid.  
- Frame Responsibility: Gallery responsible for frame condition (repair/replace); frame returns if declined by customer.  
- Installment/Layaway: Monthly payments; max 6 months without artist consent.  
- Trust: Works held in trust for artist, not subject to gallery’s creditors.  
- Termination: By written notice; 30-day window for artist to collect works.
