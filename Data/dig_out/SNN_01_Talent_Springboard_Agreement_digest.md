SUMMARY  
This is a one-year, non-employment development agreement between Shack Entertainment Limited (the Company) and a Talent, outlining a rotation of scheduled assignments for presenting, anchoring, interviews, and promotional appearances. The Talent retains freelance status, owns their pre-existing work, and receives credits and showreel rights, while the Company owns all produced content. Payment is tied to assignment completion, with strict editorial and conduct standards. Termination is possible for material breach or graduation from the arrangement.

MONEY  
Fees paid per assignment via Rate Card; paid within 7 days of recording/publication. Company expenses over £50 require Talent’s written consent. No success fee.

DATES & DEADLINES  
Term: 1 year, no automatic renewal. Graduation: 60 days’ written notice post-acceptance of staff/exclusive role. Termination for material breach: 14-day cure period. Cancellation within 48 hours: fee payable or forfeited. Accounts settle within 30 days.

OBLIGATIONS  
Talent: Reasonable availability, original/ethical content, compliance with editorial standards, no breach of assignments.  
Company: Schedule assignments, pay fees, provide facilities, publish with credit, maintain archive, promote Talent’s work.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
Talent may be “graduated” and lose rights if taken by another employer; 48-hour cancellation forfeits fees. Termination for breach risks being without payment or showreel rights. No recourse for non-renewal or unpaid assignments beyond termination. No automatic compensation for missed assignments.

KEY TERMS  
“Graduation”: Termination upon employment with broadcaster/publisher.  
“SNN Archive”: All recorded content under assignments, owned by Company.  
“Rate Card”: Basis for assignment fees.  
“Material breach”: Repeated unexcused failure to attend, violation of editorial standards, or other serious breaches.  
“Showreel licence”: Perpetual, royalty-free use of assigned content for personal promotion.
