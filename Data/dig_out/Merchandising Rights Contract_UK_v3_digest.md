SUMMARY  
This is a merchandising rights agreement between an artist and a merchandising company (likely a label) for the UK territory, granting the company exclusive rights to manufacture, distribute, advertise, and sell merchandise featuring the artist’s name, likeness, and property, with a 2-year initial term and annual extension options. The company pays the artist 20–50% of gross receipts (not net), depending on artist status, and the artist receives an advance that is recoupable from royalties. The agreement includes accounting, indemnity, warranty, and jurisdiction provisions, with the company obligated to account monthly and allow audits upon request. The artist waives third-party liability claims in exchange for the rights, and the company retains rights to block third-party exclusives if notified.

PARTIES  
None

MONEY  
- Artist receives 20–50% of gross receipts (based on artist status: 20–30% for mid-tier, 30–35% for established, 35–40% for super-groups).  
- Company pays artist 20% of gross receipts (excludes VAT, duties) as stated in clause 2, but drafted to be 50% of net receipts — likely a misstatement (actual % of gross likely applies).  
- Advance: unspecified amount; recoupable from artist’s share of gross receipts; not returnable.  
- Artist is owed monthly, with payments only if >£200 cumulative.  
- Company must pay audit costs and interest if underpayment exceeds 10%.

DATES & DEADLINES  
- Agreement date: ___ day ___ of 2016.  
- Initial term: 2 years.  
- Extension options: 1-year each, exercisable by Company at discretion prior to expiry.  
- Artist may seek better offer from third party at end of term, but grants “Matching Right” to Company.

- Accounting: 15th of each month, with remittance if >£200.  
- Audits: allowed once per year, max 2 years old, excluding prior audited periods.

OBLIGATIONS  
- Company: exploit market to maximize sales; approve all merchandising use; pay royalties; account monthly; stop merchandising upon third-party notice.  
- Artist: grant exclusivity; indemnify Company; provide legal advice; ensure no third-party liability; waive claims for misuse of property; not liable for third-party copyright violations; not obligated to pay unrecouped advances.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- Royalty % based on gross receipts is higher than net, which may be misleading or disadvantageous.  
- Advance is recoupable — artists may be forced to pay back if revenue is low.  
- No guarantee of revenue — advance is based on prediction, not certainty.  
- No “buyout” clause — artist must keep rights open for competing merchandisers.  
- Artist has no control over how merchandising is marketed or distributed.  
- No mention of minimum sales requirement or performance milestones.  
- Artist has liability for third-party claims even if artist-owned property is involved.  
- No mention of exclusivity duration or whether term ends with album or tour.  
- Company may withhold payments if audit reveals underpayment — no guarantee of payment.  
- Jurisdiction is England & Wales — may limit international protections or dispute resolution.

KEY TERMS  
- Territory: UK only (defined as “effective range of merchandising company”).  
- Merchandise: All products with artist’s property — T-shirts, posters, books, etc.  
- Property: Name, likeness, biographical material, trademarks, etc.  
- Net Receipts: Gross receipts minus expenses (not including overheads).  
- Gross Receipts: All money received from merchandising, including VAT, duties, subsidies.  
- Advance: Recoupable from artist’s share of gross receipts.  
- Matching Right: Artist allows Company to match third-party offers.  
- Indemnity: Artist holds Company harmless from artist’s breaches.  
- Accounting: Monthly, 15th; remittance if >£200.  
- Audit: Annual, 2-year limit.  
- Jurisdiction: England & Wales; mediation/arbitration allowed.  
- Exclusivity: Not transferred to third party, unless notice is received.  
- Term: 2 years + optional 1-year extensions.
