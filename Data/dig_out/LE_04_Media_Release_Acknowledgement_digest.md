SUMMARY  
This document is a signed media release and acknowledgment form, executed alongside the Springboard Partnership Agreement, which formalizes the artist’s consent to allow Shack Entertainment Ltd. to capture and retain rights to recordings and media of their performances, while retaining ownership of their original songs/art. It also acknowledges a one-year fixed commitment and requires the artist to adhere to production schedules and professional standards.
