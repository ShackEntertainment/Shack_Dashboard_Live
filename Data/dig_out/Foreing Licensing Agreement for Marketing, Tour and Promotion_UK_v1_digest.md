SUMMARY  
This foreign licensing agreement permits a licensor (typically an artist manager or independent label) to engage a licensee (a foreign-based marketing, tour, or PR company or individual) to perform promotional and tour-related services in a designated foreign territory. The agreement is for one year, with optional 3-month exclusivity extensions contingent on mutual agreement. The licensee receives 30% of net profits generated from services, but must absorb all associated costs and pay all earnings to the licensor. At termination, a 20% royalty accrues for up to 12 months post-termination. Key obligations include return of all materials upon termination, non-sublicensing, and strict compliance with IP ownership terms.

PARTIES  
Licensor: [Name of Licensor] – typically an artist manager or independent label with copyright and booking rights.  
Licensee: [Name of Licensee] – foreign-based company or individual performing marketing, tour, or PR services.

MONEY  
Commission: 30% of Net Profits (defined as revenues minus currency/transfer fees and artist-related expenses). Post-termination: 20% royalty for up to 24 months.  
Payment: All income received by licensee must be remitted to licensor; no deductions allowed.  
Accounting: Quarterly statements within 15 days; timely payments of net profit share upon invoice receipt.

DATES & DEADLINES  
Effective Date: Date of last signature.  
Term: One year, renewing annually unless terminated with 3 months’ written notice.  
Exclusivity Trial: 3-month exclusive period; extendable upon mutual agreement.  
Post-Term Royalty: Applies 20% for up to 2nd anniversary.  
Inspection Rights: Once per 12-month period, 30-day written notice.  
Mediation/Arbitration: Must be resolved via agreed mediator before arbitration.

OBLIGATIONS  
Licensee must:  
- Perform Services as agreed (touring, PR, promotion).  
- Use licensed materials only for promotional purposes.  
- Return all materials (artwork, passwords, media) within 30 days of termination.  
- Absorb all costs (travel, manufacturing, etc.) from commission.  
- Remit all income received to licensor.  
- Avoid breaches of warranties or conflicts with performance obligations.

RISKS & RED FLAGS  
- No protection for licensee’s IP rights; all IP retained by licensor.  
- High commission (30%) may be unsustainable if revenues are low or volatile.  
- No warranty that exclusivity will be extended; risk of early termination.  
- No partnership status; separate legal entities.  
- No explicit “force majeure” or “confidentiality” clauses.  
- Limited recourse if performance falls short; reliance on mediation/arbitration.

KEY TERMS  
- Net Profits: Revenues minus costs (currency, travel, manufacturing, artist expenses).  
- Territory: Designated foreign region for services.  
- Exclusive Trial Period: 3 months, extendable.  
- Intellectual Property: Licensor retains all rights; licensee has no interest.  
- Mediation/Arbitration: Required before legal action; confidential; binding.  
- Successors & Assignees: Provisions bind future entities.
