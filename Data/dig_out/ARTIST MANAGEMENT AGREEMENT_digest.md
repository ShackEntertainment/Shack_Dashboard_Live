**SUMMARY**  
This document is a comprehensive Artist Development Management Agreement between Valeria Del Prete (the Artist) and Bola Kassim of Shack Entertainment Ltd. (the Manager), effective October 25, 2012. The agreement establishes the Manager’s fiduciary role in guiding the Artist’s career, with authority to act as attorney-in-fact and sign agreements, while the Artist is bound to pay 20% of net monthly earnings (excluding specified exclusions) to the Manager and is responsible for reimbursing expenses up to £100/month. The contract includes two 2-year auto-extendable options, termination triggers based on failure to secure a Major Label Agreement (90-day notice) or Manager’s non-involvement (30-day cure notice), and a 1-year sunset clause requiring 10% gross monthly payment post-termination. The agreement is governed by UK law, requires written modifications, and includes confidentiality, fiduciary duties, and no partnership status. No financial compensation or fees are explicitly disclosed in the document, and no third-party payments or audits are covered.

- Manager: Bola Kassim, Shack Entertainment Ltd. (London, UK)

**MONEY**  
- Artist pays Manager 20% of net monthly earnings (excluding royalties, advances, commissions, non-entertainment income, and other exclusions)  
- Post-termination (for 1 year), Artist pays 10% of gross monthly income  
- Artist authorizes third parties to remit funds directly to Manager  
- Manager may deduct loans/advances from Artist’s earnings  
- Manager may reimburse self for expenses up to £100/month (no cap above this)  
- No explicit fees or compensation mentioned for Manager’s services — no payment structure defined

  - Cooperate fully with Manager; exert best efforts to advance career
