SUMMARY  
Shack operates under two immutable principles: no artist is charged to perform, and all talent is paid on time within seven days of each event. Pricing for all lanes—including venue splits, sponsorships, advertising, revision rounds, charity discounts, presenter fees, interview commissions, guest appearances, and crew rates—will be determined by the Managing Director (MD) and the Revenue Analyst (RA) once the platform is live. All pricing structures must be consistent with Shack’s commitment to fair, transparent, and artist-friendly operations.
