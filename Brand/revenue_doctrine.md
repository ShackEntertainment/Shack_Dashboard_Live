# SHACK REVENUE DOCTRINE — v1 (04/09/2026)
1. Test: who is the product? Artist → 70/30 on their work. Shack brand → 100% Shack.
2. B2B income (sponsorship, endorsement, promotion, advertising,
   Shack-branded and co-branded merch) is Shack's sole income:
   growth, cost recoupment, annual intake pipeline.
3. Artist-work income splits 70/30 AFTER documented event costs
   (backline, equipment, staff, security, paneling) are recouped.
4. Acts paired with sponsors are paid fees at the Shack Ethos rate
   card — a number, not a negotiation.
5. Merch stall: stand staff paid first; artist-mark-led sales 70/30;
   Shack-mark-led or co-branded sales 100% Shack.
6. No free ride, no extraction.
7. Every contract states the separation explicitly.
8. Marketplace and payment fees are recouped from gross before the 70/30 split on online sales.
9. Artist-run outlets report monthly: order export or read-only shop access. The 30% is only as auditable as the data behind it.
